Use your college account to log in. Your username is your staff or student number, not your email address.

First time here? Your account is created automatically once enrolment is confirmed by the registry. This can take up to two working days after you accept your offer. If you have accepted and still cannot log in after that, contact the IT service desk rather than creating a second account.

Forgotten your password? Use the reset link on this page. The reset email goes to your college address only — personal addresses are not held on the account, for security reasons.

Net-new: magic link

The one thing in this prototype with no core equivalent. Moodle has no passwordless login: login/token.php is web-service tokens, and auth/email only confirms a new account. The nearest existing mechanic is the forgot-password flow, and it is the right shape to build on — user_password_resets stores a 32-character token per user with timerequested, expiry comes from $CFG->pwresettime (1800s), and core_login_process_password_reset() (login/lib.php:84) already emails a one-time URL and deliberately does not reveal whether the account exists. A login link is the same three parts with a different landing page. Anything real would still have to answer: does it bypass MFA (tool_mfa), and is it offered per auth plugin or site-wide?

Where the instructions go

auth_instructions is an admin_setting_confightmleditor with no length limit (admin/settings/login.php:92-97), and it is one of the first things a site fills in. Today it replaces the welcome panel (theme/boost/layout/login.php:31 → login_panel.mustache:52-56), vertically centred inside a panel that is sticky at exactly 100vh (login.scss:22-32) — so anything past the fold cannot be scrolled to. Because that panel is d-none below 992px, the same content is rendered a second time inside the form for small screens (loginform.mustache:133-137).

The centred design splits it by length instead of by breakpoint. A short note sits inline above the fields, where it is read before anyone types. The long version goes outside the login box, below it: the box keeps its position on the page however much a site writes, and the copy flows underneath at the same width — no panel to outgrow, no bound, no second copy for mobile. Both regions are contenteditable here, so paste your own site's text in and see where it lands.

Removing the background image

An option on today's design, and a proposal rather than a setting. theme_boost_get_extra_scss() (lib.php:58-89) always injects a background image for .login-layout-left: the admin's loginbackgroundimage if one is set, otherwise the shipped pix/login_background.jpg plus an "AI-generated image" watermark. There is no way for a site to say "no image".

With it off, the panel is white and the translucent copy box goes with it — that box only exists to lift white text off a photo, so without the photo it is just a grey rectangle over nothing. The copy becomes ink and the layout is otherwise untouched. With it on, the prototype drops in one of five inline-SVG landscapes at random, standing in for the admin's upload; switching the option back on picks another. That variety is the point — the panel has to hold up against an image nobody on the Moodle side chose.

Same shell, other pages

pagelayout = login is not just this page: sign-up, forgot password, digital minor, age/location verification and MFA all render in the same shell (login/signup.php:103, login/forgot_password.php:51, login/digital_minor.php:54, login/verify_age_location.php:57, admin/tool/mfa/auth.php:38). Anything changed in core/login_layout or core/login_panel changes all of them — signup is a real moodleform with a dozen fields, which is worth holding in mind when choosing between the two shells.
